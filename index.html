About GeoBrad.dev

GeoBrad.dev is my geospatial consultancy, started in 2024. I build open-source mapping and spatial analysis tools for nonprofits, researchers, and community organizers who have geographic data but no budget for a GIS team.

I have over 15 years of geospatial work behind me, a master's in Spatial Data Science from Penn State, and a master's in International Relations. Most of what I build runs on React, Django, PostGIS, Leaflet, and CesiumJS.

What I usually build is an interactive map, a spatial dashboard, an automated data processing job, or a reusable WebGIS template an organization can run on its own. I spend a lot of time on the interface, since an analysis nobody can operate is not much use.

The intended users are environmental groups tracking pollution, civic groups working on voter access, researchers looking at infrastructure risk, and organizers working on spatial justice.

Most of this is pro bono. Everything I write is open source so organizations can keep it running after I hand it off.

If you have a mapping problem and nobody to hand it to, get in touch.
